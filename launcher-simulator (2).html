Launcher Trajectory Simulator

A 2D projectile launcher that integrates the equations of motion under gravity and optional quadratic air drag using 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4). Adjust mass, launch angle, and muzzle velocity to see the flight path, altitude, and speed evolve in real time — no external libraries, everything on this page runs client-side.
